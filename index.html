HIFZ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

HIFZ is a Qur'an memorization app. This policy describes what the app does with information on your device. It reflects how the app actually works.

HIFZ has no user account and no sign-in. It contains no analytics, no crash reporting, and no advertising. There is no cloud sync. We do not operate a server that receives your personal information, and we do not sell or share personal information with anyone.

What the app stores on your device

  • Memorization progress, bookmarks and notes
  • App settings, including language, reciter and prayer-calculation preferences
  • Your most recent location coordinates, so prayer times and the Qibla direction still work without a network connection
  • Any recitation audio you choose to download, stored unencrypted in the app's own storage

Removing the app removes all of it. We have no copy, because none of it is sent to us.

Permissions the app asks for

Location

Used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Your most recent position is saved on your device so the app works offline.

Converting your coordinates into a place name is performed by your device's own geocoding service, provided by your operating system vendor (for example Google on Android, or Apple on iOS). That lookup is handled by your device and is subject to your operating system vendor's privacy policy.

Location is not sent to us, and it is not used for advertising or profiling.

Microphone

Used only on the Recite & Check screen, and only after you start recording.

HIFZ does not record your recitation to a file and does not keep what you recited. The speech is converted to text by your device's built-in speech recognition service. On most Android devices that service sends the audio to its provider in order to transcribe it. That processing is performed by your operating system vendor and is subject to their privacy policy.

HIFZ itself never uploads your voice anywhere.

Notifications

Used for prayer alerts and reminders, when you enable them. They are scheduled locally on your device. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver them.

Network connections the app makes

HIFZ contacts one service of its own:

  • everyayah.com — the source of Qur'an recitation audio. Playing or downloading a recitation requests audio files from that service. As with any web request, that service can see your IP address and what you requested. HIFZ sends no account information, because there is none.

In addition, your device's own geocoding and speech-recognition services may make network requests as described above. Those are operated by your operating system vendor, not by HIFZ.

Children

HIFZ does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Third-party software

HIFZ is built with open-source components. Their licences are listed in the app under About HIFZ → Third-party notices. These are software libraries compiled into the app; they are not services that receive your data.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it does with information, this policy will be updated at the same time.
