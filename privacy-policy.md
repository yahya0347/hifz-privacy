# HIFZ — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 14 September 2026

HIFZ is a Qur'an memorization app. This policy describes what the app does
with information on your device. It reflects how the app actually works.

## The short version

HIFZ has **no user account and no sign-in**. It contains **no analytics, no
crash reporting, and no advertising**. There is **no cloud sync**. We do not
operate a server that receives your personal information, and we do not sell
or share personal information with anyone.

Your memorization progress, bookmarks, notes and settings are stored by the
app on your own device.

## What the app stores on your device

- Memorization progress, bookmarks and notes
- Your app settings, including language, reciter and prayer-calculation
  preferences
- Your most recent location coordinates, so prayer times and the Qibla
  direction still work without a network connection
- Any recitation audio you choose to download, stored unencrypted in the
  app's own storage

Removing the app removes all of it. We have no copy, because none of it is
sent to us.

## Permissions the app asks for

### Location

Used to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. Your most recent
position is saved on your device so the app works offline.

Converting your coordinates into a place name is performed by your device's
own geocoding service, which is provided by your operating system vendor
(for example Google on Android, or Apple on iOS). That lookup is handled by
your device and is subject to your operating system vendor's privacy policy.

Location is not sent to us, and it is not used for advertising or profiling.

### Microphone

Used on the **Recite & Check** and **Muraja'ah** screens, and only after you
start listening.

HIFZ does not record your recitation to a file and does not keep what you
recited. The speech is converted to text by your device's built-in speech
recognition service. **On most Android devices that service sends the audio
to its provider in order to transcribe it.** That processing is performed by
your operating system vendor and is subject to their privacy policy.

HIFZ itself never uploads your voice anywhere.

### Notifications

Used for prayer alerts and reminders, when you enable them. They are
scheduled locally on your device. Nothing is sent to a server to deliver
them.

## What works without a network

The Qur'an text, its translations and the prayer-time calculation are built
into the app. Reading, memorizing, the Qibla direction and prayer times need
no connection and send nothing anywhere.

## Network connections the app makes

HIFZ requests audio from two services. It has no server of its own:

- **everyayah.com** — Qur'an recitation audio. Playing or downloading a
  recitation requests audio files from that service.
- **cdn.islamic.network** — spoken translations, when you turn them on in
  Listen.

As with any web request, those services can see your IP address and what you
requested. HIFZ sends no account information, because there is none.

In addition, your device's own geocoding and speech-recognition services may
make network requests as described above. Those are operated by your
operating system vendor, not by HIFZ.

## Children

HIFZ does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including
children.

## Third-party software

HIFZ is built with open-source components. Their licences are listed in the
app under **About HIFZ → Third-party notices**. These are software libraries
compiled into the app; they are not services that receive your data.

## Changes to this policy

If the app changes what it does with information, this policy will be
updated at the same time.
